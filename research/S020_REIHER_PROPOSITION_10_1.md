## CLM-0109 — Proposition 10.1

Every **cloudy** length-(2p−1) sequence of indexed points in F_p² containing four points in **general position** is **simple**. Cloudy means total sum zero with no nonempty proper zero-sum subset; simple is the previously established rank-two extremal structural class, detected by p−1 pairwise dependent original positions (S014 Observation2.6). The four original indexed points A,B,C,D in Definition9.3 general position yield 14 pairwise DISTINCT subset sums T_l=(r_l,s_l) indexed by the four singleton, six pair, and four triple subsets, and the space V of leading five-tuples of degree-four polynomials vanishing on all 14 has dim V<=2. S019 Lem9.6 provides a sufficient geometric condition but is not silently assumed here.

**Reduction and exact multiplicity.** Prior S014 settles p=2,3. Work p>=5. Assume cloudy P=(A,B,C,D,P_1,...,P_(2p−5)) is not simple. Since P'=(P_1,...,P_(2p−5)) has at least p indexed terms, all equal would furnish p identical points forming a forbidden proper zero block. Thus at least two residual points are distinct; by S014 Cor2.5 they are linearly independent. A single invertible linear map sends two ORIGINAL residual positions to E=(1,0), F=(0,1); cloudy/simplicity and quartic general-position dimension remain invariant under GL2.

**Quartic annihilator and moment interpolation.** W=V^perp⊆F_p^5 has dim>=3. For α=(α40,α31,α22,α13,α04)∈W, append ten zeros to obtain z∈F_p^15. Let b_l be the evaluation column at (r_l,s_l) for the 15 monomials of total degree<=4. If a linear functional annihilates all b_l, its coefficient polynomial vanishes at all 14 points, hence its leading quartic coefficient belongs to V, so the functional annihilates z. Double-annihilator linear algebra therefore gives z∈span(b_1,...,b_14), i.e. some (not necessarily unique) γ_1,...,γ_14 with Σ_l γ_l r_l^m s_l^n=0 for m+n<4, and =α_mn for m+n=4. No interpolation determinant or division is silently assumed.

**Indicator polynomial and indexed deletion equation.** Define Q(x,y)=Σ_l γ_l((r_l+x)^(p−1)−1)((s_l+y)^(p−1)−1). Since binomial(p−1,t)=(-1)^t in F_p and p−1>=4, all total degrees above 2p−6 vanish. The homogeneous part of degree 2p−6 is α40 x^(p−5)y^(p−1)+α31 x^(p−4)y^(p−2)+α22 x^(p−3)y^(p−3)+α13 x^(p−2)y^(p−4)+α04 x^(p−1)y^(p−5). For every ORIGINAL residual index j, delete precisely P_j; the 2p−6 other residual positions are (g_i,h_i). Substitution x=Σ ε_i g_i, y=Σ ε_i h_i yields a polynomial in 2p−6 Boolean variables of degree<=2p−6. A nonzero full squarefree coefficient forces a nonzero value at some ε∈{0,1}^(2p−6) by the independently proved S014 coefficient lemma. But Q nonzero implies one T_l plus a subset of residual terms outside j equals zero: in F_p each factor u^(p−1)−1 is nonzero iff u=0. This is nonempty (uses at least one distinguished term) and proper (j and at least one distinguished term omitted); cloudiness prohibits it. Thus the squarefree coefficient is zero.
