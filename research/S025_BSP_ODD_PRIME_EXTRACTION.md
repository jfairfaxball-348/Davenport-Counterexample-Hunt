## CLM-0120: independently reconstructed original p-or-3p existence and weaker short extraction (PROGRAMME_PROVED with source-stated Olson input)

Assume no p- or 3p-term zero-sum in S, aiming at contradiction. For any indexed T of length m>=4p−3, apply Chevalley–Warning to the four polynomials in m variables x_i:
 F_0=Σ_i x_i^(p−1), and F_j=Σ_i a_{ij}x_i^(p−1), j=1,2,3, where a_i=(a_{i1},a_{i2},a_{i3})∈F_p^3.
Sum of degrees is 4(p−1)<4p−3<=m. Chevalley–Warning says number of simultaneous zeros is divisible by p. A support I that is a zero sum with |I| divisible by p corresponds to (p−1)^|I| nonzero choices and contributes (−1)^|I| mod p. Since p is odd, the sign is (−1)^j on |I|=jp. Thus the EXACT alternating congruence is 1−N_p(T)+N_{2p}(T)−N_{3p}(T)+N_{4p}(T)−N_{5p}(T)+... ≡0 mod p; all N_j vanish when jp>|T|.

First exclude N_{5p}(S): if a zero-sum Z⊂S of length5p existed, choose any indexed T⊂Z of length4p−3. Since T has neither p nor3p zero sums and length<4p, Chevalley–Warning yields 1+N_{2p}(T)≡0 mod p. Therefore N_{2p}(T)>0 as a nonnegative integer; select a zero-sum Y⊂T of length2p. Then Z\Y has length3p and zero sum, contradiction. Hence N_{5p}(S)=0.

Put x=N_{2p}(S), y=N_{4p}(S) mod p. Chevalley–Warning on S yields 1+x+y≡0. For every T⊂S of length4p−3, 1+N_{2p}(T)≡0; sum over all such T to get binom(6p−3,4p−3)+binom(4p−3,2p−3)x≡0, i.e. 10+3x≡0. For every U⊂S of length5p−3, 1+N_{2p}(U)+N_{4p}(U)≡0; sum to get binom(6p−3,5p−3)+binom(4p−3,3p−3)x+binom(2p−3,p−3)y≡0, i.e. 5+3x+y≡0.

All FIVE binomial reductions are valid for every odd p including p3 and p5 by (1+t)^p≡1+t^p and base-p digits p−3: binom(6p−3,4p−3)≡binom(5,3)=10; binom(4p−3,2p−3)≡binom(3,1)=3; binom(6p−3,5p−3)≡binom(5,4)=5; binom(4p−3,3p−3)≡binom(3,2)=3; binom(2p−3,p−3)≡binom(1,0)=1. The high digit reductions remain valid when p=3/5; explicitly p3 gives 10≡1 and 3≡0, so the second equation alone reads 1=0; p5 gives x=0 from the second, y=0 from the third, contradicting the first. Universally subtract (1+x+y)=0 from (5+3x+y)=0 to obtain 4+2x=0. For odd p, x=−2; substituting into 10+3x=0 gives 4=0 mod p, impossible for an odd prime. So S HAS a zero sum Z of length p or 3p.

If |Z|=p, it is already <=(3p−1)/2. If |Z|=3p, choose 3p−2 of its positions. By classical source-stated Olson D(C_p^3)=3p−2, there is nonempty zero-sum Y among those positions, hence Y is a proper nonempty subset of Z. Since Z is zero-sum, Z\Y is also nonempty zero-sum. One of their sizes is <=floor(3p/2)=(3p−1)/2. Therefore EVERY (6p−3)-term indexed C_p^3 sequence for odd p admits a nonempty zero sum of length <=(3p−1)/2.
