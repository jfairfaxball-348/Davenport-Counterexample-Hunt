## CLM-0201(a): the precise 4,094 outsider-shadow theorem

Excluding A=∅ and A=X leaves **4,094** nonempty proper original-index subsets. Each has h_A=iota^{-1}(Σ_{x∈A}x) in H. Then the following are EQUIVALENT:
(1) Every nonempty proper G-zero subset respecting all 22 fixed original pairs is absent.
(2) U is H-zero-sumfree, ΣT=0, and for EVERY parity-zero original outside subset A with ∅≠A≠X, h_A ∈ E(U).
Here “respecting pairs” means taking both a_i,b_i or neither, not splitting a pair. To show (1)⇒(2): if h_A=−Σ_{i∈B}u_i for some indexed B⊆[22], A plus both original members of B is a nonempty proper zero-sum subset (|A|≤14, hence ≤58 originals); so h_A∈E(U). U itself is zero-free. Conversely for any respecting subset, partition as A and B. If ∅≠A≠X the extension membership precludes zero. If A=∅, zero-freeness of U precludes all nonempty B; if A=X, total-zero gives h_A=c, and h_A+Σ_{i∈B}u_i=−Σ_{i∉B}u_i, zero only when B=[22] (the entire T). Thus all proper respecting cases are excluded.

More explicit exact census when q≠0: the 7-singleton parity map has 16 subsets C with XOR0 (empty, full, seven Fano lines and seven complementary caps), and 16 subsets with XORq. The 8 g original-index subsets J have 128 even and 128 odd choices. So 2,048 parity-zero configurations are (J even,C XOR0), of which empty and full are the two exceptions; the OTHER 2,048 are (J odd,C XORq), ALL proper and genuine. When q=0, all 256 J can be paired with any of the 16 XOR0 C, giving the same 4,096 total. This strictly exposes the earlier omission of g-only even J, all-seven+C with even J, AND the 2,048 mixed-odd J and XORq singleton subsets. The prior 14 Fano line/cap tests for every even J cover only part of this census.

Complement symmetry: for parity-zero A, h_{X\A}=c−h_A.
